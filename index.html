How it works

  • These are Unicode look-alike characters, not real fonts. A “bold” A here is a different character (U+1D400) that merely looks bold. That is why the styled text works anywhere plain text works — bios, usernames, captions, chats — but it is not a font you can install or change the size of.
  • Some apps and devices may not render every character. Older phones and some apps show a box or a blank for the rarer blocks (Cherokee, runes, braille, double-struck digits). Bold, italic, script and fullwidth render almost everywhere; the exotic ones are worth a quick check in the app you plan to use.
  • A few styles cannot cover every letter. Unicode has no superscript Q and no subscript B, C, D, F, G, Q, W, Y or Z — those letters stay plain instead of disappearing. Superscript and subscript digits work in full.
  • Your favourites are saved in this browser only. Star a style and it stays pinned at the top on this device. Your text is never saved, uploaded or sent anywhere — reload the page and the text box is back to the sample.
  • Copy-all copies one line per style in the form “Name: styled text”, so you can paste the whole list somewhere and pick from it later.
